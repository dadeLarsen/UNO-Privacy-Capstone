You do not know which model wrote each rewrite; judge each one on its own, against the passage.

For every rewrite and every key point, choose one verdict:

- partial: the rewrite conveys the point but loses or blurs a part that matters: a condition, an exception, a scope word, who does it, or a significant item from a list.

- contradicted: the rewrite says something the point rules out, for example reversing a promise, turning "may" into "will" or "does not knowingly" into an absolute promise, or adding an exception or condition the passage does not make.

Passage:
One of the main ways in which we collect information from third parties is when you sign in with a Third-Party Service like Facebook or Apple. When you log into our mobile application through your Facebook account, you will be asked to enter your Facebook email address and password. Facebook controls the information collected from you during the log-in process; PLAYSTUDIOS does not control the information that Facebook collects from you. When you use any of our Applications through Facebook or on a mobile Application that you have connected to Facebook, we will have access to, and may collect and store, the following information: your public profile, profile picture, gaming profile (your player name and avatar), gender, list of friends who play the same apps that you play, date of birth, email address, and IP address. We may use this information to (i) send you email; (ii) facilitate your log in for apps; (iii) render pronouns and personal your experience based on gender; (iv) display your first name or player name and your player avatar in-game. If you make a purchase in our Facebook game, we will also collect your IP address and country, and you submit a dispute through Facebook, your dispute email address and any comment you submit. For information about how Facebook may disclose your information, including any information you make public, please consult the Facebook Privacy Policy. We store the information that we receive from Facebook along with other information that we collect from you or receive about you. When you use Sign in with Apple you will be asked to enter your Apple ID and the email address you use with your Apple ID. When you first sign in using Sign in with Apple, you can elect to hide your email address. For information about how Facebook may disclose your information, including any information you make public, please consult the Facebook Privacy Policy. We store the information that we receive from Facebook along with other information that we collect from you or receive about you.

Key points:
1. Signing in with a third-party service such as Facebook or Apple is a main way the company gets information from third parties.
2. (critical) Facebook controls the information it collects during Facebook log-in; PLAYSTUDIOS does not control it.
3. (critical) Through Facebook, PLAYSTUDIOS may collect and store your public profile, profile picture, gaming profile, gender, friends who play the same apps, date of birth, email address, and IP address.
4. It may use this to email you, ease log-in, personalize your experience by gender (such as pronouns), and show your name and avatar in the game.
5. For purchases in its Facebook game it also collects your IP address and country, and for disputes, your dispute email address and comments.
6. (critical) It stores information received from Facebook together with other information it collects or receives about you.
7. (critical) With Sign in with Apple, you can choose to hide your email address when you first sign in.
8. Facebook's Privacy Policy explains how Facebook may disclose your information.
