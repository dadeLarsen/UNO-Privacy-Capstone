Passage:

3. (critical) Through Facebook, PLAYSTUDIOS may collect and store your public profile, profile picture, gaming profile, gender, friends who play the same apps, date of birth, email address, and IP address.

We may use this to send emails, help you log in, personalize your experience based on gender, and show your name and avatar in the game.
